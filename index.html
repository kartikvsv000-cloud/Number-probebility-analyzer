How The Analyzer Works

1. Recent history is analyzed using multiple windows: 20, 50, 100 and 200 results.

2. The system checks frequency, recent trend, previous-number transitions, two-number sequences and skip-one patterns.

3. Big/Small probability combines these statistical signals instead of relying on only one pattern.

4. Strength is shown as Strong, Medium or Weak depending on the difference between the two probabilities and the amount of available data.
